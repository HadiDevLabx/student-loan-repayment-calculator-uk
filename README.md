# Student Loan Repayment Calculator UK — All Five Plans

> On Plan 2 with a £35,000 salary you repay £505.35 a year, about £42.11 a month.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

9% of everything **above** the threshold, not 9% of everything.

| Plan | Who | Threshold | Rate |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Plan 1 | before Sept 2012, and NI | £26,900 | 9% |
| Plan 2 | Sept 2012 to July 2023 | £29,385 | 9% |
| Plan 4 (Scotland) | Scottish students | £33,795 | 9% |
| Plan 5 | English courses from Aug 2023 | £25,000 | 9% |
| Postgraduate Loan | master's and doctoral | £21,000 | 6% |

Plan 5 has the **lowest** threshold of them all, so a Plan 5 borrower repays more than a Plan 2 borrower on the same salary.

It is charged on **gross pay**. Your personal allowance shelters none of it.

## What this does not cover

Each plan is written off after 25 to 40 years. Most borrowers never clear the balance, so voluntary overpayment usually buys nothing. Check where you stand before overpaying.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
